Buying a plot is an exciting milestone, but the legal registration process in Telangana can often feel overwhelming for first-time investors. Thanks to the introduction of the **Dharani Portal** and streamlined registration systems, buying legal, approved plots has never been safer or more transparent.

Here is the exact step-by-step process you will follow when purchasing a plot in Telangana.

## Step 1: Document Verification (Due Diligence)

Before exchanging any money, the most critical step is verifying the title.
You must obtain the **Encumbrance Certificate (EC)** for the last 30 years. The EC proves that the property is free of legal disputes, mortgages, and that the seller actually owns it.

Additionally, verify the **Link Documents** (the history of previous owners) and ensure the layout has final approval from HMDA or DTCP.

## Step 2: Drafting the Sale Deed

Once the documents are verified and the payment terms are agreed upon, a legal professional drafts the **Sale Deed**. This document contains:
* The exact dimensions and boundaries of the plot.
* The agreed sale consideration (price).
* Details of the buyer and seller.
* The history of the title transfer.

## Step 3: Paying Stamp Duty and Registration Fees

To register the property legally, you must pay government taxes. In Telangana, the typical breakdown is:
* **Stamp Duty:** 5.5% of the property value.
* **Registration Fee:** 0.5% of the property value.
* **Transfer Duty:** 1.5% of the property value.

These are calculated on the *Market Value* (government rate) or the *Consideration Value* (actual sale price)—whichever is higher.

## Step 4: Slot Booking via Dharani / IGRS

Gone are the days of standing in lines for hours. You must now book a slot online via the **IGRS Telangana** portal (for non-agricultural lands) or the **Dharani Portal** (for agricultural lands). You select a date and time to visit the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO).

## Step 5: Visit the Sub-Registrar Office

On the day of registration, the buyer, seller, and two witnesses must be present at the SRO.
1. The Sub-Registrar verifies the identities (Aadhar/PAN) of all parties.
2. Biometrics (fingerprints and photos) are captured.
3. The original Sale Deed is scanned into the government database.
4. You receive a legally binding registered document bearing the official seal and document number.

## Step 6: Mutation

Registration transfers the title, but **Mutation** updates the revenue records (changing the name on the property tax and municipal records). With the integrated systems in Telangana, mutation is largely automated for approved layouts, but it's always critical to follow up and ensure your name reflects on the official Dharani/Municipal records.
